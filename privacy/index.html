Privacy Policy

Last updated October 23, 2026

Great Minds does not require an account. You can play together on one device or use a game code to connect two devices.

Information used for online games

For an online game, we process the game code, optional player nicknames, answers, and round status so both players can play and see the result. Temporary device-specific room tokens keep players in the correct game.

Online room records expire 24 hours after they are created and are deleted by automated cleanup that runs every five minutes. Answers in Pass & Play are handled on the device during the game and are not sent to the online game service.

Service information

Our hosting provider may process technical information needed to deliver and secure the online service, such as IP addresses, request times, and error logs, under its own operational retention practices. Hosting this website may involve similar technical logs. Great Minds does not use advertising or third-party analytics SDKs in the app.

How information is used

We use game information to run games, troubleshoot problems, and protect the service. We do not sell player information. Online room data is processed by Supabase, our service provider. If you contact support, we use the email and information you provide to respond to you.

Your choices

You can avoid online room processing by using Pass & Play. To ask about your information or request deletion, contact gmta.contact@gmail.com. Include the game code if available; do not send the private room token. Because online games have no accounts and room data is temporary, we may be unable to identify a particular game without its code. We may need to verify a request before disclosing or deleting information.

Children and sensitive information

Great Minds is intended for a general audience. Please do not enter sensitive personal information as a nickname or answer.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy as the app changes. The current version will be posted here with its updated date. Questions can be sent to gmta.contact@gmail.com.
