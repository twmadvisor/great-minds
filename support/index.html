Support

Need help with a game code, audio, a bug, or a privacy question? Email us at gmta.contact@gmail.com.

What to include

Tell us your device type and briefly describe what happened. If the problem involves an online game, the game code can help us investigate. Please don't send private information or another player's answers unless they're needed to explain the issue.

Playing together

For one-device play, choose Pass & Play. For two-device play, one player creates an online game and shares the six-character code with the other player.
